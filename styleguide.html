01
基本理念Principles

The palette comes from architecture diagrams: pale tints that separate layers, coloured hairlines that mark edges, and one indigo wire that shows how things connect. Colour tells you which layer you're in. It is never there for decoration.

No.RuleDetail
1One colour per layerEach section owns exactly one tint and its matching border.
2Tint for areas, border for edgesNever fill an area with a border colour, except for small markers.
3Indigo only connectsUse it for links, arrows, focus states and the main button. Nothing else.
4Grey means codeDates, versions and identifiers sit in grey monospace chips.
